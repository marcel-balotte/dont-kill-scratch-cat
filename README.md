# Don't Kill Scratch Cat! 🛹

A skateboarding game developed in Scratch as my first project for Harvard's CS50x (Week 0).

## Play Online

🎮 [Click here to play Don't Kill Scratch Cat!](https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/1389626164/)

## About the Game

The objective is simple: jump over Scratch Cat by pressing the space bar without hitting him!

Each successful jump increases your score. Try to beat your high score!

## Features

- Four difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, and Very Hard
- Hand-drawn skateboarding animations
- Moving background
- Collision detection
- Score and high score tracking
- Background music with an on/off toggle
- Game Over sound effects

## How to Play

1. Click **Start Game**.
2. Wait for the countdown.
3. Press **Space** to jump over Scratch Cat.
4. Avoid collisions and try to beat your high score!
